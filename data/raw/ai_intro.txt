Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines.
AI systems are designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence such as
visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn and improve from
experience without being explicitly programmed. Deep Learning is a further subset of ML
that uses neural networks with many layers to model complex patterns in data.

Key areas of AI include:
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Computer Vision
- Robotics
- Expert Systems
- Reinforcement Learning
